## Aim  
To demonstrate the ability of text-to-video generation tools to reproduce an existing video by crafting precise prompts, and to identify key elements within the video to generate a result as close as possible to the original.  

---

# Procedure

## 1. Analyze the Original Video

Identify and note the following key elements:

* **Objects/Subjects**:
  Chocolate brownies, ingredients, mixing bowl, melted chocolate, baking tray, oven, sliced brownies, kitchen tools.

* **Colors**:
  Dark chocolate brown tones, warm golden lighting, beige wooden countertop, rich glossy chocolate highlights.

* **Textures**:
  Smooth melted chocolate, thick brownie batter, soft fudgy brownie interior, glossy chocolate surface.

* **Lighting**:
  Warm indoor cinematic lighting with soft shadows and highlights.

* **Background**:
  Indoor modern kitchen setup with a clean and aesthetic baking environment.

* **Composition**:
  Close-up ingredient shots, top-angle mixing scenes, slow-motion pouring shots, cinematic brownie slicing shots.

* **Style**:
  Realistic cinematic food-commercial style with smooth transitions.

---

## 2. Create the Basic Prompt

**Basic Prompt:**

"Write a simple description of the video (e.g., “A homemade chocolate brownie being baked in a cozy kitchen”.

---

## 3. Refine the Prompt with More Detail

**Refined Prompt:**

Add specific attributes such as color, lighting, mood, and environment (e.g., “A cinematic homemade chocolate brownie video with rich dark chocolate batter, warm golden kitchen lighting, soft steam from freshly baked brownies, and gooey fudgy texture in a modern cozy kitchen”).

### 3. Prompts Used  

**Basic Prompt:**
 
 Create a realistic cooking video showing homemade chocolate brownies from scratch. Include mixing ingredients, preparing the batter, baking the brownies in the oven, and cutting the brownies into pieces. Warm kitchen lighting, smooth camera shots, delicious chocolate texture, simple and cozy baking atmosphere.
  
**Refined Prompt:**

  Create an ultra-realistic cinematic food video of rich homemade chocolate brownies made entirely from scratch in a modern premium kitchen. Begin with aesthetic ingredient shots featuring butter, dark chocolate, cocoa powder, eggs, sugar, flour, and vanilla arranged beautifully on a wooden counter. Show detailed close-up scenes of melting chocolate and butter together, whisking eggs and sugar until smooth and glossy, folding thick chocolate batter slowly, and pouring it into a parchment-lined baking tray. Capture satisfying slow-motion shots of the brownies baking in the oven with warm golden lighting and soft steam effects. End with cinematic close-ups of slicing soft fudgy brownies with gooey melted chocolate inside, topped with chocolate chunks and cocoa dust. Smooth transitions, shallow depth of field, food commercial style, luxury bakery aesthetic, cozy background ambiance, professional cinematography, ultra-detailed textures, Instagram reels style, 4K realism.
